▸ Text normalization: leetspeak mapping, spacing collapse, repetition reduction

Room DB • AnalysisHistoryDatabase
Historical threat ratio from past scans. DANGER=1.0, WARNING=0.5, SAFE=0.0. Flat average (no time decay).

STAGE 3 — ENSEMBLE SCORING & GUARD RAILS
▸ Weighted Sum: uS×0.259 + nS×0.296 + bS×0.185 + tS×0.148 + rep×0.112
▸ Short-link Floor: URL shortener + urgency text → BODMAS floor at 0.5
▸ Corroboration Gate: 1 model alone → capped at 69 (WARNING max). 2+ → uncapped
▸ Exemptions: Typosquat ≥0.9 or Tamil heuristic ≥0.70 bypass gate
▸ Cloud Guard: Local SAFE + cloud DANGER → capped at 25–38
▸ Disagreement Detection: std > 0.35 + no model > 0.70 → UNCERTAIN
⬇
STAGE 4 — CLOUD AI FALLBACK (Gemini)
▸ Trigger: Score 35–65 (disagreement boundary). Below/above = local-only.
▸ Fallback Chain: gemini-2.5-flash → 2.5-flash-lite → flash-latest → 3.5-flash → 3.5-flash-lite → 3.1-flash-lite
▸ Blending: 55% local ML + 45% cloud AI. Temperature 0.0 (deterministic).
▸ Privacy: OTPs/PINs/CVV masked before transmission. 5s timeout. 10min cache.

STAGE 5 — VERDICT DECISION

WARNING 40–69
DANGER 70–100

🛡️ 7 GUARD RAILS
1
Corroboration Gate: Single model alone cannot push to DANGER or WARNING. Requires 2+ independent signals.
2
Gate Exemptions: Confirmed typosquat (≥0.9) or Tamil heuristic (≥0.70) bypass the single-model cap.
3
Bank/Retailer NLP Cap: Legitimate bank SMS or retailer promos cap NLP contribution at 0.22.
4
Cloud Corroboration Guard: When all local models say SAFE, cloud alone cannot produce DANGER.
5
Short-link + Urgency Floor: URL shortener combined with urgency text → BODMAS floor at 0.5.
6
Ultra-clean Local Guard: ML <15% + no corroboration + cloud DANGER → capped to 39.
7
Email Quality Gate: Message must be ≥15 chars and ≥3 words before sending email alert.
